Why me, specifically

Most people have done one of these jobs. I've done all four.

Early-stage companies need someone who can sell the idea, build it, put structure around it and carry it into a new market. Usually that takes several hires. I've done each one, for real, with numbers to show for it.

  1. 01

    I can sell.

    Four years as a client advisor at Louis Vuitton in Vienna. I ranked in the top 10% nationally, beat my targets by 8–15% and raised client retention by 18%. I know how a demanding buyer decides.

  2. 02

    I can build.

    I founded GZ Trading, a B2B wholesale company, and ran it end to end: pricing, suppliers, customers and a team of seven. Now I'm building 吃了吗 on my own, from the first line of code to the Google Play listing.

  3. 03

    I can structure.

    At KPMG Financial Services Advisory I turned regulatory requirements into dashboards and recommendations for bank leadership. I also onboarded eight new colleagues and cut their ramp-up time by about 30%.

  4. 04

    I can cross over.

    I grew up between Austrian and Chinese culture and have since studied and worked in Vienna, Seoul, Lisbon and Kingston. I don't need a translator to read a room in Europe or in Asia.

So when an idea has to move from one market to another, from slide to product, or from product to paying customer, I've already stood on both sides of that gap.

What I'm building

吃了吗？ Chi Le Ma

“Have you eaten?” is how people in China say hello. Chi Le Ma teaches Chinese the way people actually speak it.

  • From zero to HSK 9, with separate starts for beginners, learners who already know some Chinese and heritage speakers who grew up hearing it at home.
  • Jun, an AI conversation partner living in Shanghai, who chats and role-plays at your level.
  • Characters stroke by stroke, plus spaced review and graded stories, so what you learn stays.

Designed, written and shipped by me, using AI coding tools. It's now in closed testing on Google Play.
